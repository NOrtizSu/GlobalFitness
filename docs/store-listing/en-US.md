## Full description (max. 4000)

GlobalFitness turns your Bluetooth bathroom scale and your fitness band into one simple, private overview of your health: weight and body composition, steps, sleep and heart rate, all on your phone.

SMART SCALES
• Supports 66 Bluetooth scale models from brands such as Xiaomi, Renpho, Eufy, Yunmai, Beurer, Sanitas, Etekcity, Omron and more.
• Automatic detection: step on your scale with the app open and it is recognised. Scales that need a connection are only set up after you confirm they are yours.
• The Xiaomi Mi Body Composition Scale 2 is fully supported and tested with real hardware. Other models are marked "Experimental": they follow published protocols but have not all been tested with a real scale yet.
• Weight-only scales work too, and you can always add your weight manually.

BODY COMPOSITION
• BMI, body fat, fat mass, muscle, skeletal muscle (when the scale provides it), body water, bone mass, visceral fat, basal metabolic rate, metabolic age, protein, ideal weight and body type.
• Values reported by the scale itself, or calculated with published formulas for each scale family.
• Trend charts with a 7-day moving average, a normal-range band, your target weight, weekly rate and a goal projection.
• History grouped by month, with the details of every measurement.

HEALTH CONNECT
• See the steps, distance, calories, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep stages, blood oxygen (SpO2), respiratory rate and workouts that apps like Mi Fitness, Zepp or Samsung Health write to Health Connect.
• Optionally send your scale measurements to Health Connect so other apps can use them.
• Data read from Health Connect stays on your phone: it is never uploaded or used for advertising.

SMART BANDS AND WATCHES
• Works with any band or watch whose app writes to Health Connect, including Xiaomi and Redmi bands through Mi Fitness and Amazfit / Zepp devices.
• Experimental direct mode for the Xiaomi Smart Band 9: live heart rate, find your band, alarms and notification forwarding, without Mi Fitness.
• Support for standard Bluetooth heart-rate devices and open watches such as PineTime and Bangle.js is in development.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE
• No account needed: by default everything stays on your phone.
• Optional Google sign-in with cloud backup of your profile, measurements and scales, to restore them on a new phone. You can delete the backup and the account from the app at any time.
• Export and import your measurements as CSV.

ADS
• The free version shows ads. A one-time "Remove ads" purchase removes them for good — no subscription.
• Your health and body data are never used for advertising.

LANGUAGES

IMPORTANT
GlobalFitness is not a medical device. Body composition values are estimates from bioelectrical impedance and published formulas; they are for general fitness and wellness only and are not a diagnosis. Talk to a health professional for medical advice.
